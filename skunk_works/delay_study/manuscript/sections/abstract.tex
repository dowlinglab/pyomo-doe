The Temperature Control Laboratory (TC-Lab) provides transient heater and sensor
data for testing parameter estimation and experiment design. We compared thermal
models with an $n$-stage input-delay chain in physical conductance--capacity
coordinates and in gain--time-constant coordinates. The study separates model
family, parameter coordinates, initialization, and numerical integration. An
explicit transfer-function calculation shows that the original four-parameter,
two-state thermal model has a one-dimensional physical-parameter equivalence
class under the observed output and prescribed initial conditions. The cold
one-stage formulation represents this family with three parameters and a
pole-label ambiguity, and also admits a repeated-pole limiting case.
A constant input with equilibrated delay states
cannot identify the delay. The completed baseline comprises 99 multistart fits;
the draft's uncertainty, model-adequacy, and prospective-design results will be
updated from their saved manifests as those phases finish. All results in this
working draft await human scientific review.
