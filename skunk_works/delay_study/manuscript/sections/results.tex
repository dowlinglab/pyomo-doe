\section{Results}
The baseline comparison completed all 99 cases. The profile phase completed
297 of 300 jobs on its first pass. Three original-model conductance profiles
exhausted their nuisance-optimization budgets; their failed status is retained
pending a documented retry. None of the completed profiles found a minimum
requiring a baseline refit under the recorded tolerance.

The manuscript's generated tables and figures will replace this working status
after the profile retries, resampling, sensitivity analyses, and input-design
calculations have been audited. Intermediate fitted values are available in the
saved result files and are not yet presented as final uncertainty conclusions.
